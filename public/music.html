BAFENYI

A song I did with Mobile Worship, a worship movement I co-founded in 2015. Bafenyi means Victor or Conqueror — a reminder that no matter the circumstances we face, we remain in the identity of the victorious.

Mobile Worship ft Kutlo

“Botlhe Ba Ga Jeso ke Bafenyi” means those who belong to Christ are victors.

“Ba fenya ka lebitso la gagwe” means they conquer in His name.

This is the unofficial version I did with Mobile Worship — carrying the message of identity, victory, and belonging in Christ.
