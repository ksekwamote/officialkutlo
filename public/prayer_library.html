THIS WEEK'S PRAYERS

28 June

New Week Prayer

calendar_month

I command the days ahead of me to align themselves with the will of God.

Monday — I command you to hear the voice of the Lord. Every promise God has spoken concerning my life shall begin to find fulfillment in you.

Tuesday — I declare that the favor of the Lord shall surround me like a shield. Doors that no man can shut shall be opened before me.

Wednesday — Let the glory of the Lord shine upon my life. Let His light expose every hidden blessing and every opportunity prepared for me.

Thursday — I command you to obey the Word of the Lord concerning my destiny. Every divine appointment assigned for me shall not pass me by.

Friday — You belong to the Lord. I declare victory over every battle, success in every assignment, and peace in every decision.

Saturday — Let it be a day of restoration, refreshing, and renewed strength. May the joy of the Lord fill my heart, and may I testify of His goodness.

Sunday — I dedicate you to the Lord. May I encounter His presence in a fresh way. Let my spirit be renewed, my faith strengthened, and my heart anchored in Christ.

Father, I declare that every day of this week shall work together for my good.

No weapon formed against me shall prosper. No evil shall overtake me. No plan of the enemy shall prevail.

This week shall testify of Your goodness, Your mercy, and Your faithfulness.

In Jesus' mighty name, Amen.

29 June

Lord Protect My Family

shield

Lord, protect my family. Let your presence stand guard over us, and let no weapon formed against us prosper. Contend with those who contend with us. Fight for us, O God, and let us hold our peace.

Every altar raised against my family, every assignment from the pit of darkness, be destroyed and canceled in the name of Jesus.

I come against every evil pattern operating in my bloodline — patterns of untimely death, patterns of sickness, patterns of disappointment, poverty, stagnation, unemployment, and delay.

By the authority given to me in the name of Jesus, I declare that it ends with me. I plead the blood of Jesus over the doorposts of our home. Let every spirit of destruction pass over us. None of us will die before our time.

We will live to see the goodness of the Lord in the land of the living. We will live to fulfill our destinies.

Father, let peace rule in our household. Let unity dwell among us. Let joy be our portion.

Let success follow us wherever we go. Let everything our hands touch prosper. For you have declared in your word, you will be saved, you and your household. And by the authority of that word, I declare my family is saved. My family is covered.

30 June

As I Enter July

wb_sunny

I refuse to enter the second half of this year carrying the weight of the first.

July, hear the voice of the Lord concerning me — I will not leave this month the same way I entered it.

As I step into the second half of this year, I declare that the grace of God goes before me. Every disappointment, every delay, every unanswered prayer from the months behind me shall not define the months ahead of me.

Father, I thank You because You are the God of new beginnings. What I could not accomplish in the first half, let Your hand accelerate in the second. Redeem every wasted opportunity. Restore every lost season. Let July become the turning point of my testimony.

I command this month to align with the will of God concerning my life. July, you will not work against me — you will work for me. Every day shall carry favor. Every week shall produce fruit. Every opportunity ordained by Heaven shall locate me.

I decree that I will not walk in fear, confusion, stagnation, or defeat. I walk in wisdom. I walk in discernment. I walk in divine health, supernatural provision, and uncommon peace.

Father, let every closed door that carries my name be opened. Let every promise You have spoken begin to manifest. Let July be marked by answered prayers, divine encounters, supernatural acceleration, and undeniable breakthroughs.

I enter this new season with boldness, not because of my strength, but because the Lord goes before me. The second half of this year shall be greater than the first. I will laugh again. I will testify again. I will see the goodness of the Lord in the land of the living.

In Jesus' name, Amen.

01 July

Lord Protect My Children

family_restroom

Lord, protect my children. Cover them under the shadow of your wings. Shield them from the hidden dangers of this world, from what is seen and what is unseen.

Guard them from negative influences, from sudden tragedies, and from anything sent to harm them. With all that is happening in the world today, I set these ones apart before you, Lord.

Keep them for yourself. Preserve their lives until they have fulfilled their purpose in this world. I anoint them with the oil of gladness. Let nothing steal their peace. Let nothing rob them of their innocence.

Father, I pray that you protect my sons and daughters from every predator, from those who would seek to harm them physically, emotionally, or sexually.

Let your presence be their defense. Let your hand be their covering. Let no evil come near them or before them.

I pray that you guard their minds, their hearts, and their destinies. Silence every voice that seeks to lead them astray, voices that would corrupt, confuse, or misdirect them.

Let no influence steal their purpose or plunge them into darkness. Lord, I cover these ones with the promises of Psalm 91, and I declare that with long life you will satisfy them and show them your salvation.
